# Manufacturer-specific Rivet demos

## Positioning

Rivet automates drawing-review work around each manufacturer's equipment,
engineering standards and CAD conventions. Start with a returned drawing package:
capture comments, prepare cited responses, compare revisions and export the log.
Engineering edits are introduced for defined change types after the manufacturer
supplies its design rules, component details and representative completed jobs.
The engineer reviews the proposed files and evidence before approving them.

Say: **“The review workflow is shared. The engineering is built around how your
company actually designs and builds its equipment.”**

This is the product direction, not a claim that Rivet already implements every
manufacturer's engineering process. Do not promise 90% coverage, universal CAD
editing, automatic UL compliance or autonomous engineering release.

## Larson: five-minute path

1. Open its supplied returned drawing and a readable comment. Explain that this
   is the real package, processed with permission. The annotation inventory is
   28 native text comments; this is not an engineering-accuracy percentage.
2. Show a prepared response and its cited drawing/specification page. The NEMA
   enclosure question is useful: distinguish what the reviewer requests from
   what the specification prints. A conflicting requirement remains a decision.
3. Download the Excel or Word comment matrix. Proposed responses are populated;
   open statuses and engineer-review requirements remain visible.
4. Open Drawing redesign. Show NLTC → DETC before/after and the proposed native
   DXF. Explain that this run changes seven terminology labels, finds two
   documentation requests already present, and leaves 26 requests open. It does
   not complete a physical or electrical redesign. A composite comment remains
   open even when its naming clause is changed.
5. Show the independent preservation/re-read result and the open request
   register. Ask the engineer to select one recurring change family to build and
   validate next. The current, unaddressed Outlook review draft contains the
   refreshed Excel/Word/PDF logs and native text-change review report. The
   evaluation operator prepared this delivery and verified the attachments;
   it is not an unattended attachment-selection demonstration. The earlier
   PDF-only message is preserved separately. Download the full native DXF from
   the private order; it is too large for an inline attachment in this path.

Do not open Requirements, Configure, Release, the general agent or phase-conversion
experiments. No actual Rev B or manufacturer BOM has been supplied for this
package, so there is no real before/after revision-cycle or BOM-correction claim.
The newer CAD may already include requests from the marked PDF; do not count
those as changes made by Rivet.

## South Coast: discovery, then a short demonstration

Open SCC-DEMO-20261022 in the owner's demonstration workspace. Say immediately:
**“This control-panel example is fictional. We'd use a redacted historical SCC
project to establish what works on your files.”**

1. Ask where returned drawings cost the most time: reading marks, assigning
   questions, making edits, checking resubmittals, or preparing the response log.
2. Open Drawing and comments. Show a drive-sizing request, the SCCR question,
   a substitution, and the excluded surge-protection request. Open the original
   PDF and its specification citation rather than scrolling through every row.
3. Show the distinction between a requested change, an engineering question and
   potential added scope. SCCR, substitutions and wiring require the engineer's
   decision. Demo prices come from explicitly fictional source records.
4. Download Excel and Word. Show comment, sheet/page, proposed response,
   reference and disposition. A prepared response does not resolve a comment.
5. If useful, show the separately labelled fictional PDU revision proof: it
   catches one missed nameplate value, changes a native drawing/BOM copy and
   rechecks the saved fields. Explain the limited supported fields.
6. Close on one redacted completed revision cycle, an engineer who can score it,
   and the most repetitive engineering change worth automating first.

Use saved results during the call. Do not trigger fresh model generation, approve
engineering, connect SCC systems, or send mail as part of the demonstration.

## Answers to SCC's written questions

| Question | Accurate answer for this demo |
| --- | --- |
| PDF redlines, handwriting, clouds | Typed native annotations are supported. Scan/handwriting and graphical interpretation remain partial; show confidence and unreadable areas. No universal handwriting-accuracy claim. |
| Compare drawing revisions | Supported comparisons exist, including bounded drawing/BOM fields and visual regions. Show the labelled saved example; verify coverage on SCC revisions. |
| Sheet and component association | Source page/annotation anchors are available. Device association requires an actual identifiable tag or supported CAD evidence; some references remain uncertain. |
| Modify electrical drawings | Supported native text/attribute and BOM-copy edits exist. General physical and wiring redesign is not validated; build defined change types around SCC's CAD and rules. |
| UL 508A, substitutions, wiring | Flag for an engineer. Rivet does not certify a panel, infer SCCR from a requested value, or approve replacement components. |
| Added cost | Separate potential scope additions from questions. A price requires trusted manufacturer data; the example uses fictional rows and is not a live quote. |
| Incorrect responses | Require source references, preserve conflicts and missing inputs, and keep proposed responses separate from engineer decisions. These controls reduce risk; they are not an accuracy guarantee. |
| Exact citations | Open source-page/annotation references and supplied specification excerpts. A citation supports its particular claim, not the correctness of the entire design. |
| Excel or Word matrix | Both export paths exist. Verify the saved example's populated response cells and readable output before the call. PDF is also available. |
| Open/resolved/pending history | Revision and comment history are retained. Draft responses do not become approvals or customer acceptance. |
| Manual upload | Yes. A redacted file-only proof needs no access to SCC's mailbox. |
| NetSuite | No live SCC NetSuite connection. Job-numbered exports can be associated manually; map project IDs, records and permissions before building a connector. |
| Storage and AI use | Separate application-enforced workspaces; the present demo runs locally. Relevant content may go through the OpenAI API. API training is off by default; this is not a zero-retention or security-certification claim. See pilot-security.md. |
| Redacted historical project | Yes. Agree authorization, NDA, retention and the permitted processing first. Keep tags, ratings, revisions and comments needed for evaluation. |
| Pilot and pricing | Begin with the proposed free proof on one historical package. Agree the paid pilot's supported workflow, evaluation, term and price after that; do not imply a signed pilot or recurring revenue. |
| Approval and sending | A person reviews technical/commercial outputs and sends the customer response. No autonomous customer sending or engineering release is demonstrated. |

## What to request

- A baseline drawing, returned markup and engineer-completed next revision for
  the same job; BOM/I/O/terminal reports when relevant.
- Their CAD system, standard blocks/templates, component rules and the reference
  material engineers use to decide substitutions, wiring and panel ratings.
- Three frequent changes and one costly missed change. Ask which required
  information is usually outside the drawing package.
- An engineer reviewer and success criteria: correct comment capture, correct
  proposed answers, missed changes found, false alarms, and time saved. Measure
  extraction separately from checking and keep unseen jobs out of development.

Private customer links, file locations and receipts belong in the ignored
`.data` demo folders, not in this versioned guide.
